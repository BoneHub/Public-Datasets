BoneHub Public Datasets

A Guide for finding 3D bone shapes & medical image datasets.

📖 About

This project aims to facilitate finding and accessing publicly available datasets with 3D bone shapes or medical images (CT, MRI) from which bone shapes can be extracted. Designed for researchers in biomedical engineering, medical imaging, biomechanics, and related fields.

📌 Note: This page does not host datasets. Only links and information are provided.
